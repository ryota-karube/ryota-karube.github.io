About

I am a second-year Ph.D. student in the Doctoral Program in Geosciences at the University of Tsukuba, working on urban climate, urban meteorology, and geospatial information science. My current research focuses on enriching urban digital twins through high-resolution urban climate simulations for heat-risk assessment and the optimization of heat adaptation strategies.
