01 / Profile

About

I am a Ph.D. student in the Doctoral Program in Geosciences at the University of Tsukuba and a research intern at the GATE Institute, Sofia University. My research focuses on urban climate, urban meteorology, and geospatial information science, with particular emphasis on high-resolution urban climate modelling, heat-risk assessment, and geospatial data for urban thermal environment analysis.
